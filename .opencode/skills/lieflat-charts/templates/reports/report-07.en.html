AI Coding Culture Survey

Independent developers in China · July 2026
In July 2026 we surveyed 369 independent developers in China who use AI coding tools. We wanted three things: where they use AI, what got them started, and where they learn the craft. Respondents were 18–39, and all data is self-reported and anonymous. The numbers on this page are demo data.
