Decide who this page speaks for before you pick a layout.

Each template is a full publishable report skeleton, not a single chart. Copy the HTML, then replace the data, titles, and sources. Charts inside the page must still be chosen from catalog.md. This vendored edition ships without thumbnail previews; the upstream repo keeps them in docs/assets/reports/.

HOW TO CHOOSE

Filter by report type, audience, and reading task first; layout and density come second. Research / studies: R01, R07, R08, R11. Business data / finance: R04, R09, R12. Annual / long-cycle retrospectives: R02, R03, R06. Product, project, and personal records: R05, R10. Template names are layout clues, not industry limits.
